Waste Sorting Challenge

NYC-inspired sustainability game

Small individual mistakes can create bigger problems for the entire city system.

How to Play

🎯 Goal

Sort falling trash into the correct bins before time runs out. Keep contamination below 100%.

🖱️ Controls

Click a falling trash item to select it, then click a bin to sort it.

📦 Three Bins

♻️ Recycling — clean plastic, glass, metal, paper, cardboard

🌱 Compost — food waste, organic materials

🗑️ Landfill — dirty/mixed items, plastic bags, wrappers

⚠️ Key Rules

• Item condition matters — a greasy pizza box goes to Landfill!

• Each bin holds 10 items. Call the truck to empty instantly.

• Correct sorts speed up falling (max 200%). Wrong sorts slow it down.

📊 Scoring

✅ +10 pts · ❌ -10 pts +15% contamination · 💥 Overflow: -5/5s
